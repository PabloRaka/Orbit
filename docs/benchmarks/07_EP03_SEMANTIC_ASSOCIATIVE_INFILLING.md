$$E[\psi] = -\frac{1}{\beta} \ln \left( \sum_{\mu=1}^M \exp\left(\beta \, \text{Re}\langle \xi_\mu | \psi \rangle\right) \right) + \frac{1}{2} \|\psi\|^2$$

where:

- $\text{Re}\langle \xi_\mu | \psi \rangle = \text{Re} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \xi_\mu^*(x) \psi(x) \, dx$ is the Hilbert space inner product overlap.
- $\|\psi\|^2 = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} |\psi(x)|^2 \, dx = 1$ is the conserved unitary probability density.

### 2.2 Dissipative Gradient Flow with Soft Context Clamping

During associative recall, a partial cue (e.g. `kucing:`) occupies the prompt region $x \in [x_{\text{start}}, x_{\text{prompt\_end}}]$. The unobserved suffix region is left free to evolve.

The field evolves under dissipative gradient descent driven by soft context clamping:
$$\frac{\partial \psi(x, \tau)}{\partial \tau} = -\frac{\delta E}{\delta \psi^*(x)} + \alpha P_{\text{prompt}}(x) \left( \psi_{\text{prompt}}(x) - \psi(x, \tau) \right) + \xi(x, \tau)$$

where:
1. **Attractor Pull**:
   $$-\frac{\delta E}{\delta \psi^*(x)} = \sum_{\mu=1}^M w_\mu \xi_\mu(x) - \psi(x), \quad w_\mu = \frac{\exp(\beta \, \text{Re}\langle \xi_\mu | \psi \rangle)}{\sum_{\nu=1}^M \exp(\beta \, \text{Re}\langle \xi_\nu | \psi \rangle)}$$

   $$P_{\text{prompt}}(x) = \frac{1}{2} \left[ 1 - \tanh\left( \frac{x - x_{\text{prompt\_end}}}{\sigma} \right) \right]$$
